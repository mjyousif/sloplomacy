# Map Visual QA Playbook

This skill defines the visual quality inspection criteria for the Sloplomacy SVG cartography canvas (`EuropeMap.tsx`). Use it when evaluating screenshots generated by Playwright, `e2e_runner.py`, or visual regression suites.

## 1. Unit Anchors & Centroids (`mapLayouts.ts`)

Every army and fleet token must center accurately on its assigned anchor:
- **Centroid Coordinates**: Compare piece centers with `unit` points defined in `frontend/src/data/mapLayouts.ts` and `frontend/src/data/europeMapGeometry.ts`.
- **Style Invariant**:
  - In Classic style (`letters`), `CLY.unitPos` intentionally lies offshore in the North Atlantic to preserve pixel-fidelity with the 1901 original map.
  - In fitted styles (`seated`, `flags`, `nameplate`), `CLY` is brought ashore.
- **Visual Defect Signs**:
  - Unit token clipped by province polygon boundaries or displaced into an adjacent sea/land zone without explicit coast orders.
  - Text labels or supply stars directly colliding with or obscuring unit badges.

## 2. Bicoastal Fleet Placement

Three provinces feature split coasts with separate Fleet waterways. Armies always use the land centroid; Fleets must use their explicit coastal anchor:

1. **Spain (`SPA`)**:
   - `SPA/NC` (North Coast, Bay of Biscay): Anchor at `(x: 226.0, y: 1008.7)`.
   - `SPA/SC` (South Coast, Western Mediterranean): Anchor at `(x: 228.5, y: 1184.8)`.
2. **St. Petersburg (`STP`)**:
   - `STP/NC` (North Coast, Barents Sea): Top shoreline anchor.
   - `STP/SC` (South Coast, Gulf of Finland): Baltic inlet anchor.
3. **Bulgaria (`BUL`)**:
   - `BUL/EC` (East Coast, Black Sea): Eastern coastal anchor.
   - `BUL/SC` (South Coast, Aegean Sea): Southern coastal anchor.

**Inspection Rule**: In visual captures of orders involving split coasts, verify that a fleet ordered to/from a split coast renders its token and coastal tag (`NC`, `SC`, `EC`) on the correct shoreline, never at the inland army centroid.

## 3. SVG Movement Arrows & Support Vectors

Order visualizations drawn by `map/layers/orders/` (`OrderArrowLayer.tsx`, `SupportArrowLayer.tsx`) must satisfy:
- **Arrow Termination**: Movement arrows must terminate cleanly at the target province centroid (or specific coast if fleet movement).
- **No Boundary Clipping**: Arrow curves, strokes, and marker heads (`<marker id="arrowhead">`) must not clip against SVG canvas bounds or container boundaries (`viewBox="0 0 1000 1300"`).
- **Support Indicators**:
  - Support-to-hold: Dashed line ending in a support node marker at the defended unit.
  - Support-to-move: Dashed vector linking supporter to the target movement vector without obscuring the primary attack arrow.
- **Convoy Vectors**: Convoy dotted arcs linking sea fleets must align end-to-end between intermediate fleets and coastlines.

## 4. Dislodged & Retreating Unit Spacing

During Retreat phases (`Spring <Year> Retreats` and `Fall <Year> Retreats`):
- **Dislodgement Offset**: When an attacking unit dislodges an existing unit, both exist in the province during the retreat phase.
- **Visual Separation**:
  - The dislodged unit MUST be visibly offset from the invading unit (rendered with a dislodgement ring, pulse indicator, or offset position).
  - The two tokens must never completely occlude one another.
- **Retreat Options**:
  - If a retreating unit is selected, valid retreat destination provinces should display distinct highlighted borders or indicators.

## 5. Visual QA Checklist for Multimodal Inspection

When viewing a test failure screenshot or E2E artifact:
- [ ] Are all unit tokens resting on valid centroids or designated coastal positions?
- [ ] For `SPA`, `STP`, and `BUL`, are fleets on the correct coast?
- [ ] Are all order vectors, arrowheads, and support dashes unclipped and clearly legible?
- [ ] Are dislodged units clearly distinct from occupying invaders?
- [ ] Does any piece or badge overflow the SVG viewport?
